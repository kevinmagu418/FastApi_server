Bean Rust (Uromyces appendiculatus)
Chemical Treatment: Apply fungicides containing chlorothalonil or sulfur at the first sign of rust. Follow label instructions carefully.
Organic Treatment: Use neem oil or sulfur-based organic sprays. Practice crop rotation and remove infected debris.
Prevention: Plant resistant varieties, ensure proper spacing for air circulation, and avoid overhead irrigation.

Potato Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
Chemical Treatment: Use systemic fungicides like metalaxyl or contact fungicides like mancozeb. Timely application is critical before infection spreads.
Organic Treatment: Use copper-based sprays (bordeaux mixture). Ensure well-drained soil and use certified disease-free seeds.
Prevention: Destroy cull piles, remove volunteer potatoes, and practice a 3-year crop rotation.
